The Programme

Story Studio is an intensive, practice-based communication training programme designed for researchers, clinicians, and industry professionals. We propose a tailored version for 10-12 cardiac surgeons at Edwards Lifesciences, delivered over 1.5 days in Switzerland. The programme is built on evidence-based communication science, Barbara Minto's Pyramid Principle, and the data storytelling methodology of Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic.

Over five sessions, participants learn to structure their science using proven narrative frameworks, present clinical data clearly and persuasively, and deliver with confidence on stage and on camera. Every session includes hands-on exercises, iterative practice, and honest, constructive feedback. By the end of the workshop, each participant will have presented multiple times, received detailed individual feedback from the trainer and their peers, and will leave with a proven framework they can apply to any future presentation or communication engagement.

Why This Matters for Edwards

With the COMMENCE trial's seminal 10-year data now published, there is a significant opportunity to ensure that the surgeons closest to this evidence can communicate its significance effectively. In our experience, when a scientific expert fails to connect with an audience, it is rarely the expertise or the science that is the issue. It is the communication. Surgeons who present their research with clarity and human connection are the ones who get invited back, who build collaborations, and who move their field forward.

This programme supports Edwards Lifesciences' commitment to advancing clinical education and evidence dissemination. We tailor each session to the specific challenges of presenting COMMENCE data, working with a pan-European group of mixed skill levels, and preparing surgeons to represent clinical evidence confidently at international congresses and professional events. We frame communication improvement as a universal life skill, not a competition, which creates a safe and productive learning environment where every participant improves from their own starting point.

What Participants Receive

All participants receive 12 months' access to the Story Studio Portal, an AI-powered communication toolkit trained on the Story Studio methodology for analysing language, creating metaphors, structuring narratives, and building presentation plans. They also receive a course handbook, a structured Content Tool template for planning any future presentation, and 30 days of email support for upcoming presentations or communication opportunities.

Following the course, Whipsmart Media provides Edwards with a summary report identifying participants who showed particular aptitude for communicating clinical evidence, along with recommendations for follow-up activities. We also strongly recommend that Edwards plans a tangible follow-up activity for participants shortly after the course, such as a recorded video, an internal panel, or an opportunity to present at an upcoming event. In our experience, this significantly increases the long-term impact of the programme.

Programme at a Glance

Participants complete pre-work two weeks before: a confidence survey, a lay abstract of their research or COMMENCE data, and 3-5 slides. Day 1 covers four sessions: Language (how visual, emotional, and logical language transform a clinical message), Story (narrative frameworks and "prisming" COMMENCE data from different stakeholder perspectives), Presenting with Data (Pyramid Principle, slide design, behaviour change framing), and On Camera and Delivery (presence, voice, and a recorded on-camera exercise with individual feedback). Day 2 (half day, close 12:30) brings it all together: each participant delivers a full 5-7 minute presentation, receiving structured peer and trainer feedback and an on-camera recording for personal review.

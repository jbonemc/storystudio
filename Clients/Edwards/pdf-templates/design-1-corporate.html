Introduction

Why Scientific Communication Matters

Edwards Lifesciences is looking to enhance the scientific communication skills of its emerging surgical leaders — enabling them to present research with clarity, confidence, and presence at international conferences and professional events.

At Whipsmart Media, we understand this unique challenge — we have been helping researchers and clinicians to communicate their science for the past 17 years. Story Studio is a programme based on evidence-based communication science, 23 years of media experience and 15 years of professional training, shaped by the feedback of hundreds of participants.

At a major European gastroenterology congress, after the keynote — delivered by a world-leading researcher — over two-thirds of the board members admitted they did not fully understand the presentation. The issue is rarely the science. It is the communication.

We have extensive experience working with the medical device and pharmaceutical sectors, including MSL training, pitch development, and spokesperson preparation. Importantly, we have worked with Edwards Lifesciences previously, providing communications training at your Limerick facility.

Core Approach

  • Evidence-based Pyramid Principle framework
  • Practical, iterative presentation exercises
  • Constructive peer and trainer feedback
  • On-camera confidence building
  • AI-powered post-training tools

Why Scientific Communication Matters

Edwards Lifesciences is looking to enhance the scientific communication skills of its emerging surgical leaders — enabling them to present research with clarity, confidence, and presence.

At Whipsmart Media, we understand this unique challenge — we have been helping researchers and clinicians to communicate their science for the past 17 years. Story Studio is built on evidence-based communication science and has been shaped by hundreds of participants across industry, academia, and healthcare.

“At a major European congress, over two-thirds of the board admitted they didn't understand the keynote — in their own field. The issue is rarely the science. It is the communication.”

We have worked with Edwards Lifesciences previously at your Limerick facility. Our programme is built around the Pyramid Principle — ensuring the most important message leads, supported by evidence beneath.

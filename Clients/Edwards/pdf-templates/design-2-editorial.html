Why Scientific

Edwards Lifesciences is looking to enhance the scientific communication skills of its emerging surgical leaders — enabling them to present research with clarity, confidence, and presence.

At Whipsmart Media, we understand this unique challenge — we have been helping researchers and clinicians to communicate their science for the past 17 years. Story Studio is built on evidence-based communication science, 23 years of media experience, and has been shaped by hundreds of participants across institutions and industry.

We have extensive experience with the medical device and pharmaceutical sectors, and have worked with Edwards Lifesciences previously at your Limerick facility. Our programme is built around the Pyramid Principle, ensuring the most important message leads, supported by evidence beneath.

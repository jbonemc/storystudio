## TECHNICAL PROPOSAL

### Overall Approach

We propose a tailored version of our established Story Studio programme, delivered as an intensive one-and-a-half-day workshop for cardiac surgeons at Edwards Lifesciences. Story Studio is highly interactive and practical, giving participants the tools and confidence to communicate clinical research in a structured and audience-centred way.

The programme combines clear, evidence-based communication principles with structured practice activities and constructive feedback in a supportive environment. By the end of the training, participants will have presented multiple times, received detailed individual and group feedback, and will leave with a proven framework and a suite of digital tools to continue developing their communication skills.

The training uses a number of different approaches, including Barbara Minto's **Pyramid Principle**: a structured methodology for organising ideas from the top down, so that the most important message leads, supported by evidence beneath. This is particularly effective for clinical and scientific presentations, where dense data can overwhelm even expert audiences.

We actively encourage participants to see Story Studio as the beginning of a journey towards more effective communication: collecting stories, structuring arguments, and connecting with audiences in a way that makes their science memorable. At Whipsmart, we tell our clients that a single good story can be used a thousand times and in a thousand ways.

In our experience, the issue is rarely the expertise of the speaker or a failure of the science. It is the communication. Surgeons who present their research with clarity and human connection are the ones who get invited back, who build collaborations, and who move their field forward. With the COMMENCE trial's seminal 10-year data now published, there is a real opportunity to help your emerging surgical leaders tell that story well: not just with data, but with the patient impact behind it.

## COURSE STRUCTURE: 1.5 DAYS

### Pre-Work (Distributed 2 weeks before)

- **Pre-course survey**: Self-assessment of communication confidence, experience, and goals. This gives us baseline metrics for measuring improvement.
- **Lay abstract**: Each participant writes a plain-language summary of their research or a dataset from the COMMENCE trial that they wish to present. This exercise immediately highlights the challenge of simplifying complex science.
- **Slide preparation**: Participants prepare 3-5 slides on their chosen topic (their own research or COMMENCE data). These will be used in workshop exercises.

---

### DAY 1 - FULL DAY

### Session 1: LANGUAGE - The Building Blocks of Scientific Communication
*Morning | 90 minutes*

We begin with introductions and set the scene: this training is an opportunity to build a foundation for better communication generally. We then explore the three types of language that make communication memorable: **visual** (language that helps us see), **emotional** (language that helps us feel), and **logical** (language that helps us believe). This is a core part of our communications training and is particularly powerful for clinicians, who tend to default heavily to logical language at the expense of engagement.

- Demonstration of how the balance of these language types transforms a clinical message
- Quick exercises: Empty Photograph (visual storytelling from data), Emotion Mapping (identifying how clinical research connects to patient experience), and Three Pieces of Data (choosing just three data points to tell a compelling story)
- Paired exercise: participants rewrite a section of their lay abstract using the language techniques and deliver it
- Group discussion and feedback

*Break - 15 minutes*

### Session 2: STORY - Structuring Your Science
*Mid-Morning | 90 minutes*

Introduction of simple, repeatable story frameworks for research communication:
- **Setup-Conflict-Resolution** (ideal for presenting new data that challenges existing practice)
- **Problem-Inspiration-Payoff** (ideal for innovation and new therapies)
- **Past-Event-Present** (ideal for presenting new directions, explaining how the clinical landscape has shifted and why a new approach is necessary)

- Exploration of creative techniques, including "prisming" (presenting the same COMMENCE data from different stakeholder perspectives: surgeon, referring cardiologist, hospital administrator, patient), employing suspense, and using the "Opposites" storytelling device
- Group activity: participants take a technical abstract and restructure it into a clear narrative using what they have learned. In pairs, they deliver this research story three consecutive times, improving each iteration through constructive and honest feedback
- Group discussion

*Lunch - 60 minutes*

### Session 3: PRESENTING WITH DATA - Slides, Structure, and the Pyramid Principle
*Early Afternoon | 90 minutes*

This session focuses on how to present clinical data in a way that is clear, compelling, and appropriate for the audience. Many researchers overload slides with data, losing their audience before the key message lands.

- The Pyramid Principle in practice: structuring a presentation from the conclusion down, not the methodology up
- Slide design principles: what to show, what to say, and how to manage the gap between the two
- The "behaviour change" framework: before building any presentation, define what you want your audience to think, feel, or do differently afterwards
- Exercise: participants restructure their pre-prepared slides using the Pyramid Principle and present a 3-minute version to a small group
- Facilitated feedback on clarity, structure, and audience connection

*Break - 15 minutes*

### Session 4: ON CAMERA AND DELIVERY
*Late Afternoon | 75 minutes*

This session covers the physical and vocal dimensions of presenting, including on-camera performance, a skill increasingly required as organisations record interviews, panel discussions, and promotional content with their clinical experts.

- Confidence, presence, and non-verbal communication: what the research tells us about how audiences perceive speakers
- Voice use: pace, pause, emphasis, and the power of silence
- On-camera exercise: each participant delivers a 60-second summary of their key message to camera, followed by playback and individual feedback
- Discussion: adapting your story to different formats (conference podium, panel discussion, internal meeting, recorded interview)
- Reflection and preparation notes for Day 2

---

### DAY 2 - HALF DAY

### Session 5: BRINGING IT ALL TOGETHER - Presentations and Feedback
*Morning | 150 minutes (plus 30 mins break)*

The final session is where everything comes together. Each participant delivers a full 5-7 minute presentation incorporating all the techniques covered: structured narrative, effective data presentation, engaging language, and confident delivery.

- Each participant presents to the full group
- Structured peer feedback: every participant provides written observations using a feedback framework
- Detailed individual feedback from the trainer on content, structure, delivery, and presence
- On-camera recording of final presentations for personal review
- Group reflection: key takeaways, personal action plans, and commitments

*Close - 12:30pm*

## POST-TRAINING SUPPORT

### Story Studio Portal
All participants retain access to the Story Studio online portal for 12 months, which includes:
- **AI-powered communication tools** trained on the Story Studio methodology, for analysing language, creating metaphors, structuring narratives, and building presentation plans
- **The Content Tool**, a structured planning template for any future presentation or communication engagement
- **Course materials and reference guides** from the workshop
- **30-day email support**: participants can reach out with specific queries related to an upcoming presentation or communication opportunity

We strongly recommend that Edwards plans a tangible follow-up activity for participants after the course: a recorded video, an internal panel discussion, or an opportunity to present at an upcoming event. In our experience, giving participants a real deadline shortly after the training dramatically increases the long-term impact of the programme.

### Post-Course Survey
A follow-up survey is distributed to all participants to measure:
- Changes in self-assessed communication confidence
- Likelihood of applying techniques in future presentations
- Qualitative feedback on the most valuable elements of the training
- Net Promoter Score

- Participants who demonstrated particular aptitude for communicating clinical evidence to diverse audiences

- Recommendations for follow-up activities or further development

## EDWARDS LIFESCIENCES CONTEXT

This programme has been designed to support Edwards Lifesciences' commitment to advancing clinical education and evidence dissemination. With the publication of the COMMENCE trial's 10-year data, there is a clear need to ensure that the surgeons closest to this evidence can communicate its significance effectively to peers, institutions, and the broader clinical community.

We tailor each session to address:

- **COMMENCE data dissemination**: Helping surgeons bring the COMMENCE trial's 10-year data to life through patient stories, clear structure, and audience-centred delivery, so that the clinical significance of the results is understood and felt
- **Pan-European group dynamics**: The programme works effectively with participants from different cultural and linguistic backgrounds, all presenting in English. We frame communication improvement as a universal life skill, not a competition, which creates a safe and productive learning environment
- **Conference and event readiness**: Preparing surgeons to present clinical evidence confidently at international congresses, panel discussions, and professional events
- **Compliance alignment**: The training is focused entirely on scientific communication skills and professional development. It is educational in nature, discipline-agnostic in its methodology, and does not promote or reference specific products. The agenda can be reviewed and refined iteratively with your compliance team to ensure full alignment with medtech regulations
- **Varied skill levels**: The programme works with mixed groups, from strong presenters who need refinement to less experienced speakers who need foundational skills. The iterative, practice-based approach ensures every participant improves from their own starting point
